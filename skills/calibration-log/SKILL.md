# Research resolution log

Record what was stated, when it was knowable, and what the primary source later showed. The existing skill name is retained for compatibility.

## Workflow

1. Record a precise research criterion, its registration time, resolution date and primary resolution source. Mark historical imports as retrospective.
2. Resolve only after the source is available. Use met, not met, or cannot be checked. Preserve the original wording and cite the document and field.
3. Keep the log append-only. Corrections supersede an entry; they never overwrite it.
4. Check whether the evidence available at registration supported the statement. Keep later restatements distinct from the originally filed version.
5. Report source gaps and unresolved criteria. Every displayed figure needs a linked, audited claim.

## Output

| ID | Registered | Criterion | Resolves on | Primary source | Resolution | Evidence claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use explicit unknown states. Keep advice and generated forecasts out of the log.

## Rules

- Never output probabilities, price targets, position sizes or trading advice.
- Never backdate, delete a miss or reinterpret the criterion after resolution.
- Never register a forecast through a tool.
- Do not treat missing evidence as a met criterion.
